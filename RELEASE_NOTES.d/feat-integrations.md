# MyFitnessPal, Withings and WHOOP, finished

- **MyFitnessPal.** Write "connect MyFitnessPal": after one paste on a computer, every meal you
  log here is copied into your MyFitnessPal food diary with the same numbers, and from there to
  Apple Health, WHOOP and anything else that reads MyFitnessPal. The last week is copied on
  connect. Food you log in MyFitnessPal yourself is never touched.
- **Withings.** Blood pressure and heart rate, visceral fat, BMR and metabolic age, and daily
  steps and active calories now come in next to weight and body composition.
- **The coach sees it.** The latest weight (with its weekly average and monthly change), body fat,
  waist, pressure and yesterday's steps are in front of the coach on every message, and the WHOOP
  day strain and calories burned next to recovery. It stops asking for numbers the scale already
  sent.
- **Fewer repeats.** A workout from WHOOP is commented on once, not again every half hour.
- **You hear when a connection breaks.** If WHOOP, Withings or MyFitnessPal stops accepting the
  login, the coach says so once (never at night) and how to reconnect. "Disconnect MyFitnessPal"
  (or WHOOP, or Withings) in the chat removes the connection.
- Connecting WHOOP or Withings no longer fails for users whose language is not English or Russian.
